About "Don't Go"

The ethical and professional basis for our cultural boycott campaigns.

Why We Say "Don't Go"

The USA-Palestine Mental Health Network respects the position articulated by our colleagues in Palestine through the Palestinian Union of Social Workers and Psychologists (PUSWP), asking for international support in their declaration of October 2, 2018. The PUSWP represents approximately 20,000 mental health professionals in Palestine.

Consistent with the PUSWP position, the USA-PalMHN has taken a public stand urging mental health organizations and clinicians not to go to Israel. We have approached individual international mental health organizations planning to hold meetings and conferences in Israel, asking them to reconsider and hold their meetings elsewhere.

The USA-PalMHN wishes to thank our sibling organization, the UK-Palestine Mental Health Network, and the advocacy organization Jewish Voice for Peace, for their tremendous support in waging these campaigns.

Do No Harm Campaign

Demanding mental health organizations uphold the foundational ethical principle.

Do No Harm

The Do No Harm Campaign calls on mental health professional organizations to refuse participation in events that normalize Israeli apartheid and the ongoing ethnic cleansing of Palestinians — in direct violation of our profession's most foundational ethical principle.

About the Campaign Do No Harm Resources
Active — 2026

Henri Parens Symposium — Do No Harm Campaign

In February 2026, USAPALMHN launched a Do No Harm Campaign in response to the Henri Parens Symposium scheduled for March 7, 2026, titled "Resilience in Response to Violence & War." The symposium featured Dr. Merav Roth, an Israeli clinician, framing the conflict as a "symmetrical trauma" — which USAPALMHN condemned as clinically inaccurate and politically harmful.
