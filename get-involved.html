Join the Network

Free, open to all mental health professionals and students.

The USA-Palestine Mental Health Network is a 100% volunteer-led professional network open to all U.S.-based mental health workers who share our anti-Zionist perspective and our mission for justice in Palestine. Membership is free.

As a member you will receive our monthly steering committee updates, invitations to reading circles and webinars, access to clinical resources, and the opportunity to connect with over 170 Outreach Representatives across the country.

Licensed psychologists, social workers, counselors, and psychiatrists
Graduate students and trainees in mental health fields
Faculty and department chairs in mental health programs
International colleagues aligned with our mission

If you know mental health workers who share our perspective, invite them to consider joining. The network grows through peer invitation and shared values.

Become an Outreach Representative

Take a more active role in the network's advocacy work.

The network currently has 170 Outreach Representatives across the United States. Outreach Representatives are active network members who help organize local events, co-facilitate reading circles, coordinate with Doctors Against Genocide Hill Days, and represent USAPALMHN in their professional communities.

If you are interested in becoming an Outreach Representative or co-facilitating a reading circle, please contact Christine Schmidt or Cheryl Qamar.

Reading Circles

Six-session facilitated reading groups on key texts in Palestinian mental health.

USAPALMHN hosts ongoing reading circles for network members and Outreach Representatives. Each circle runs for six sessions and is facilitated by steering committee members or trained Outreach Representatives. Reading circles deepen clinical and political understanding of the issues at the heart of our work.

Active — 2026

Incarcerated Childhood and the Politics of Unchilding

Explores the systematic deprivation of childhood for Palestinian youth under occupation and its psychological consequences.

Completed — Early 2026

Psychoanalysis Under Occupation

The practice of psychoanalysis and psychotherapy in the context of Israeli occupation — clinical, ethical, and political dimensions.

Completed — Early 2026

Boycott, Divestment, and Sanctions

A foundational text on the BDS movement and its implications for professional organizations in mental health and civil society.

Interested in facilitating a future reading circle? Contact Christine Schmidt or Ben Goldstein at sc@usapalmhn.org

Travel to Palestine — Mental Health Study Delegations

A unique opportunity for U.S. mental health professionals to witness conditions firsthand.

Spring 2027 Mental Health Study Delegation

The USA-Palestine Mental Health Network presents a unique 10-day study delegation to occupied Palestine, facilitated by a professional company with experience providing alternative tours to the Occupied Palestinian Territory. The delegation provides a rare opportunity to learn directly from Palestinian colleagues about the mental health impact of occupation.

You will learn about:

  • The mental health impact of occupation on everyday life
  • Trauma within families and how it affects women and children
  • Violence against children, including imprisonment and intergenerational trauma
  • Perspectives on the mental health crisis from Palestinian professional colleagues
