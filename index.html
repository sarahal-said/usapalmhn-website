About the Network

A Network Born from Necessity and Conscience

In 2016, a group of U.S.-based psychologists, social workers, counselors, and psychiatrists came together around a shared recognition: Palestinian mental health was an underserved, often silenced dimension of both clinical practice and professional advocacy.

Today, USA Palestine Mental Health Network (USAPALMHN) is a 100% volunteer-led professional network operating in all 50 states. We do not seek institutional neutrality — we seek clinical accuracy, which requires us to name the structural conditions that produce psychological harm.

Meet the Steering Committee

Grounded in Ethics. Rooted in Justice.

Our steering committee includes licensed clinicians across psychology, social work, counseling, and psychiatry — all contributing pro bono to this shared mission.

Don't Go. Stand With Palestine.
