Psychoanalytic Activist

At the intersection of clinical theory and political responsibility.

The Psychoanalytic Activist

A curated series exploring the ethical and theoretical dimensions of practicing psychoanalysis in solidarity with oppressed communities. Contributors engage with questions of countertransference, neutrality, moral injury, and the clinician's role in bearing witness to structural violence.

Explore Series

We Are Not Numbers

Amplifying the individual stories behind the statistics of Palestinian suffering.

We Are Not Numbers

A partnership with the We Are Not Numbers project — a Gaza-based initiative pairing young Palestinian writers with international mentors to tell the human stories behind the statistics. USAPALMHN curates and amplifies these narratives to help mental health professionals understand the individual faces of collective trauma.
