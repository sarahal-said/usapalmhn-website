Why We Need Information

Understanding the context behind our clinical and advocacy work.

The resources on this page are organized to assist mental health workers in understanding the historical, political, and social context of Palestine — context that is essential for ethical, justice-oriented clinical practice.

The state of Israel maintains extensive institutional organizations with the goal of discrediting criticism of Zionism, justifying its policies, and spreading its ideology as "public diplomacy" through HASBARA — literally (in Hebrew) "explanation." Some foundational planks of Zionist propaganda assert that Israel represents the best interests of Jewish people worldwide, and that Israel is a vibrant democracy reluctantly forced to adopt security measures that outsiders have no right to criticize.

The resources below offer evidence-based, clinically grounded alternatives to these narratives — drawing on international law, peer-reviewed research, journalism, film, and firsthand testimony.
