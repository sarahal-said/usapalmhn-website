Tax Deductibility

USA-Palestine Mental Health Network is a fiscally-sponsored project of Empowerment Works, a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are fully tax-deductible. You will receive a receipt for your records upon completing your donation through Givebutter.

How Your Donation Is Used

All donations are used to cover the costs of USAPALMHN's operations — including our advocacy campaigns, reading circles, webinars, delegations, and educational resources. We are a volunteer-run organization with no paid staff.

Please note that USAPALMHN does not provide direct humanitarian aid, funding for mental health programs in Palestine, or financial support to other non-governmental organizations. Our Resources page lists a number of organizations providing direct aid to Palestine.

Gift Acceptance Policy

USA-Palestine Mental Health Network accepts donations from individuals and institutions sharing our commitment to full equality, human rights, and compliance with international law. We reserve the right to decline gifts from individuals or institutions that advocate viewpoints that contradict our fundamental values — particularly viewpoints based in racism, Islamophobia, or antisemitism.
